Benefits for Property Owners

What partnering with WorkX actually looks like

From an empty floor to a fully operating, communitydriven workspace, without you managing a single desk.

Revenue

Your space, fully utilized

We design, fit out and fill your floor with a working community, so square footage that once sat empty becomes an active, incomegenerating asset, every single day. With transparent reporting and a steady pipeline of members, you get predictable, recurring income instead of the uncertainty of a traditional lease.

Operations

We run it, you don't have to

Your property joins the wider WorkX member network, founders, startups and enterprise teams already inside the ecosystem, so your floor never has to build demand from zero. Members collaborate, network and grow together, turning your space into a vibrant hub that people genuinely want to be part of, not just another address.

What We Look For

Is your property a good fit?

We partner selectively, here's what we typically look for before taking on a new property.

Ideal property profile

  • Commercial floors or buildings of roughly 3,000 sq. ft. or more
  • Located in an accessible, wellconnected business area
  • Reliable power supply and basic infrastructure in place
  • Clear ownership, or authority to lease, the space

What makes a strong partnership

  • Openness to a managed, revenuesharing model
  • Flexibility on fitout and space configuration
  • A longterm view rather than a shortterm lease
  • Willingness to let WorkX manage the daytoday
