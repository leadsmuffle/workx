Pricing FAQ

Common pricing questions

Are there any hidden charges on top of the listed price?+

No. Every plan is quoted per 8-hour shift with WiFi, tea and coffee, and commonarea access already included, so the price you see is what you pay. Addons like extra meetingroom time or guest passes are quoted separately and always confirmed with you before you book.

What payment methods do you accept?+

Credit/debit cards, JazzCash, EasyPaisa and bank transfer.

Do I have to sign a longterm contract?+

No longterm lockin is required for day passes or single seats, book only for the shift you need. If you'd like an ongoing membership, we offer flexible plans and our team will walk you through the terms before you commit to anything.

What is the difference between a coworking seat, executive room and team room?+

A coworking seat is a shared desk on the open floor, ideal for individuals. An executive room is a private, lockable office for one person or a small team who need a closed door. A team room is a larger private space sized for a full team, with its own seating. All three include the same WiFi, tea and coffee, and commonarea access.
