FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book a seat?+

Fill in the enquiry form with your requirement and team size, and our team will get back to you shortly to confirm your booking.

Are there any hidden charges on top of the listed price?+

No. Every plan on our pricing page is quoted per 8-hour shift with WiFi, tea and coffee, and commonarea access already included, so the price you see is what you pay.

What payment methods do you accept?+

Credit/debit cards, JazzCash, EasyPaisa and bank transfer.

Do you offer corporate plans?+

Yes, we offer enterprise solutions for teams above 30 with dedicated account management. Reach out via the contact form.
