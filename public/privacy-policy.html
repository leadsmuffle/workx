Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This is a plainlanguage policy describing the data WorkX's website and booking system collect, including how Google Analytics (GA4) is used. Please have it reviewed by a lawyer before relying on it as your final, legally binding policy.

Contents: Who we are · Information we collect · Google Analytics (GA4) · Cookies · How we use information · Google as a third party · Retention & security · Your rights · Opting out of Analytics · Cookie consent · Thirdparty services · Sharing information · Contact us · Policy updates

1. Who we are

WorkX Coworking ("WorkX", "we", "us") operates a coworking space at 336 G3, M Tower, Main Boulevard, Johar Town, Lahore, Pakistan, and the website at workx.pk. You can reach us at info@workx.pk or +92 321 8845311. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, why we collect it, and the choices you have, including in relation to Google Analytics (GA4).

2. Information we collect

Personal information you give us directly:

  • Contact and enquiry forms: your name, company name, company size and workspace requirement.
  • Property submission forms: your name, email, phone number and property details, if you enquire as a landlord.
  • Account registration and login: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Bookings and payments: booking details and payment information processed through our payment providers (card payments, JazzCash, EasyPaisa).
  • Newsletter signup: your email address, if you choose to subscribe.

Nonpersonal and technical information collected automatically:

  • Device, browser and operating system type.
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, and general usage patterns on our website.
  • Approximate, city or countrylevel location, derived from your IP address.
  • Your IP address, used briefly for security purposes (such as ratelimiting abusive requests) and not stored in our database.

Some of this technical information is collected automatically by our own servers, and some (as described below) is collected by Google Analytics once it is enabled on our website.

3. Google Analytics (GA4)

As of the date of this policy, Google Analytics has not yet been activated on workx.pk. This section describes how it will operate once enabled, so our practices are transparent in advance. We will update the "last updated" date above when tracking goes live.

When enabled, WorkX intends to use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google LLC ("Google"), to understand how visitors use our website, so we can improve our content, navigation and booking experience. GA4 will collect information such as:

  • Pages and sections viewed, time spent on the site, and navigation paths.
  • Device category, browser, operating system and screen size.
  • Approximate geographic location (such as city or country), derived from your IP address. Per Google's standard GA4 configuration, the IP address itself is used briefly to determine this location and is not logged or stored by Google Analytics.
  • Referral source (such as the search engine or website that brought you to workx.pk).

Our commitment on personal data: we will not configure Google Analytics to collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or other directly identifying information. We will not enable Google Analytics' UserID feature or upload customer lists to Google, and we will not enable Google Signals or ad personalization features. Google Analytics data will be used in aggregate, statistical form to understand overall visitor trends, not to identify individual people.

4. Cookies, analytics cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. We use, or plan to use, the following categories:

Strictly necessary cookies

Used today. A session cookie keeps you logged in to your WorkX account and remembers basic preferences. The website cannot function correctly without these.

Analytics cookies

Not currently set. Once Google Analytics (GA4) is enabled, Google will set firstparty cookies (typically named _ga and _ga_<containerid>) to distinguish visitors and measure site usage, as described in the section above.

Advertising cookies

We do not use thirdparty advertising, retargeting or adtracking cookies on workx.pk.

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser's settings (usually found under Privacy or Security). Blocking cookies may affect some website functionality, such as staying logged in.

5. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and follow up on booking or propertypartnership requests.
  • To create and manage your WorkX account.
  • To process bookings and payments.
  • To send you servicerelated emails (such as booking confirmations or account verification).
  • To maintain the security of our website and prevent abuse.
  • Once enabled, to understand aggregate website usage and improve our website through Google Analytics, as described above.

6. Google as a thirdparty analytics provider

Google Analytics is provided and operated by Google LLC. When active, data collected through Google Analytics is processed by Google on our behalf, in addition to being subject to Google's own privacy practices. Google may process and store this data on servers located outside Pakistan. You can read Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy, and details of how Google Analytics processes data at support.google.com/analytics. WorkX does not control how Google itself uses this data beyond the analytics service we configure.

7. Data retention and security

We keep personal information (such as enquiry, account and booking records) for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain business and financial records, and meet legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Google Analytics data, once enabled, is retained by Google for a period we configure, up to a maximum set by Google (currently up to 14 months by default for user and eventlevel data, per Google's standard GA4 settings).

Passwords are stored using industrystandard hashing (never in plain text). Our website applies standard security measures including HTTPS, rate limiting and input sanitisation. Payment details are handled directly by our payment providers and are not stored on our servers.

8. Your rights and privacy choices

  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in those emails.
  • You can decline or delete cookies through your browser settings.
  • Once Google Analytics is active, you can opt out of it entirely, see the next section.

To exercise any of these choices, email info@workx.pk.

9. How to opt out of Google Analytics

The simplest way to opt out is to choose "Reject NonEssential" in our cookie banner or Cookie Settings, described below, this prevents analytics cookies from being set in the first place. You can also opt out independently of our banner by:

  • Installing the official Google Analytics Optout Browser Addon, which prevents Google Analytics JavaScript from sharing visit data with Google, on any website.
  • Blocking cookies from thirdparty analytics domains in your browser's privacy settings.
  • Using your browser's "Do Not Track" or similar privacy signal, where supported.

Note: Google Analytics itself has not yet been activated on workx.pk (see section 3), so no analytics data is currently collected regardless of your cookie choice. Our consent system is already live and will govern Google Analytics from the moment it is switched on.

10. Cookie consent and analytics preferences

On your first visit, workx.pk shows a cookie banner with three choices:

  • Accept All allows analytics cookies (Google Analytics) in addition to strictly necessary cookies.
  • Reject NonEssential only strictly necessary cookies are used; no analytics cookies are set.
  • Cookie Settings opens a preferences panel where you can turn analytics cookies on or off individually before saving.

Your choice is stored in your browser and applied automatically on future visits, no analytics cookies are set before you make a choice, or if you choose to reject them. You can change your preference at any time using the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of every page.

11. Thirdparty services

We use a small number of thirdparty services to run WorkX:

  • Payment processors (card payments, JazzCash, EasyPaisa) to process bookings.
  • An email delivery provider to send account and bookingrelated emails.
  • A Google Maps embed on our Contact page, which may load resources from Google when you view that page.
  • Google Analytics (GA4), once enabled, as described above.

Each of these providers processes data under their own terms and privacy practices, in addition to this policy.

12. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers needed to run WorkX (such as those listed above), or where required by law.

13. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, or want to exercise any of your privacy choices, contact us at info@workx.pk or +92 321 8845311.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, including when Google Analytics is enabled on our website. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
